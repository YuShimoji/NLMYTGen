# sports_news closed topics

The following topics are closed for the initial `sports_news` lane. Do not reintroduce them as implementation ideas unless the lane is explicitly re-scoped.

## Media and asset prohibitions

- No match footage.
- No official video footage.
- No official video screenshots.
- No official video audio.
- No X/Twitter screenshots.
- No website screenshots.
- No news site screenshots.
- No official site screenshots.
- No news photos.
- No third-party photo assets in the initial MVP.
- No external image/icon packs unless explicitly reviewed and licensed.
- No team logos or official marks as decorative assets unless explicitly reviewed later.

## AI visual prohibitions

- No AI-generated athlete images.
- No AI-generated realistic people.
- No AI-generated sports match recreation.
- No AI-generated meme assets.
- No famous meme remakes.
- No generated fan faces or fake-person reaction images.
- No AI-generated thumbnails with real-person likeness.

## Editorial prohibitions

- No fabricated comments.
- No unsourced paraphrases.
- No `海外では大絶賛`-style unsourced reaction claims.
- No long verbatim copying as the body of the video.
- No Content ID-based strategy.
- No Ken Burns effect as a policy or safety solution.
- No pictogram-heavy main visual strategy.

## Allowed backend AI uses

Backend AI can support editorial work only:

- translation drafts
- source summarization
- reaction clustering
- title brainstorming
- script outline assistance
- consistency checks

The public-facing output must remain sourced text, structured data, and self-made UI cards.
